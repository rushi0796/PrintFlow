At PrintFlow, we strive to deliver flawless automated printing services. Our refund policy outlines the terms for refunds and failed transactions.

1. Failed Payment Transactions

If your payment was debited from your bank or wallet but the print order was not successfully created or confirmed, the debited amount will be automatically refunded by Razorpay to your original payment method within 4–5 business days.

2. Failed Print Jobs

In the rare event that a confirmed print job fails to print due to hardware or connectivity issues on our print bridge, your order will be automatically eligible for retry or a full refund.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds cannot be issued for user errors in document selection, incorrect file uploads, or user-selected print settings once the job has been physically printed.

4. Contact Support

For refund status updates or assistance, please email our support team at printflowindia@gmail.com.
