At PrintFlow, document security and customer privacy are our absolute highest priorities.

1. Immediate 2.5-Second Document Deletion

All customer-uploaded files (PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, DOC, DOCX, TXT) and temporary print copies are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers 2.5 seconds after the physical print job is completed by the printer.

2. Data Encryption & Security

Uploaded documents are encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS and held strictly in temporary isolation for the duration of the print queue dispatch.

3. Zero Permanent Storage

We do not store, archive, inspect, or share customer documents under any circumstances. Once printed, documents are unlinked and erased permanently.

4. Contact Support

For any privacy concerns or queries, please reach out to our team at printflowindia@gmail.com.
